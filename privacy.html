Zagout Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Zagout ("the game") is published by the Zagout team, Bengaluru, India. Contact: viveksingh1219@gmail.com.

What the game stores

Your level progress, stars, hints and settings are saved on your device. When online features are available, the game also creates an anonymous player account using Google Firebase so you can appear on leaderboards. It stores your display name, avatar choice, level, stars, streak, weekly points and daily puzzle time, plus a copy of your progress so you can restore it.

Signing in

You can choose to sign in with Google or Apple. We then receive a user ID and, if you allow it, your name and email address from that provider. We use them only to save and restore your progress. You can sign out at any time in the Profile screen, and you can ask us to delete your account and data by emailing viveksingh1219@gmail.com.

Leaderboards

Your display name, avatar, stars, streak, weekly points and daily puzzle time are visible to other players on the leaderboards. Choose a display name that doesn't identify you if you prefer to stay private.

Purchases

If you buy No Ads, the purchase is handled by Google Play or the App Store. We use RevenueCat to check whether your purchase is active. We never see your card or bank details.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app usage information. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

On iPhone, the game asks for your permission before your advertising ID is used for tracking. You can change this any time in Settings. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. If you are in a region that requires consent (such as the EU or UK), the game shows a consent form before personalised ads are shown.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
